# Gentlemen’s Barber
Gentlemen’s Barber is a mobile application designed to modernize barber shop services. It provides customers with a seamless way to register, book appointments, manage services, and track payments, while offering staff and administrators tools to manage operations.

## Key Features of The Gentlemen's Barber

### Register & Login
- Customers create accounts with personal details.  
- Secure login validates credentials and loads the personalized dashboard.  

### Booking System
- Customers select a service, barber, and time.  
- The system checks availability and confirms booking.  
- Bookings can be rescheduled or cancelled.  

### Services
- Customers browse available services (Haircut, Beard Trim, Shave, etc.).  
- Admins can add new services, which update instantly in the service list.  

### Payments
- Customers add products or services to their cart.  
- Payment is processed, and a digital receipt is generated.  

### Feedback
- Customers submit feedback, which is stored and acknowledged with confirmation.  

### Reports
- Admins generate operational or financial reports, viewable on screen or downloadable as PDF.  

### Appointment Management
- Staff mark appointments as complete, updating the schedule and freeing slots for new bookings.

## Product Store
Customers can purchase grooming products directly within the app. Available items include:
- Shaving Cream  
- Professional Clippers  
- Hair Conditioner  
- Beard Balm  
- Facial Cleaner  
- Barber Cape  

### How It Works
1. **Browse Products** – Products are displayed in a catalog with images, descriptions, and prices.  
2. **Add to Cart** – Customers select items and add them to their cart.  
3. **Checkout** – Payment is processed, and a digital receipt is generated.  
4. **Track Purchases** – Purchases are logged and can be viewed in the customer’s profile or expense history.


## Tech Stack we implemented in the application
- **Frontend:** Android XML layouts with Material Design.  
- **Backend:** Kotlin with SQLite database(Firebase will soon be implemented connecting the official website with the application).  
- **Persistence:** SharedPreferences for user profile data.  
- **Reporting:** PDF generation for admin reports.  

## Our end goal with this app
Gentlemen’s Barber was developed to improve customer experience for the barbershop by:
- Simplifying appointment booking.  
- Providing digital receipts and confirmations.  
- Allowing staff and admins to manage services and schedules efficiently.  
- Giving customers visibility into their spending habits.
